## Case Study Positioning

Metro Ads Services should present this as a **repeatable client-delivery framework**, not merely a poster-design assignment.

### Business problem

Single Souls 30+ needed a recognizable identity, consistent event storytelling, high-intent CTAs, and a practical route from social viewership to registration.

### Metro Ads response

Metro combined creative direction, brand consistency, event communication, CTA architecture, WhatsApp handoff, registration routing, payment support assets and measurement planning into one delivery system.

### Client value

The deliverable gives the event manager reusable campaign assets, clearer audience journeys, trackable conversion points, and a structured foundation for future city/event editions.

### Repeat-job opportunity

The same operating model can be reused for monthly single-social events, city-to-city community events, curated networking evenings, interest-based social clubs, professional mixers, and partner/co-branded gatherings.

**Metro Ads Services proposition:** one operating system that can be cloned for each event, city, audience and venue.
